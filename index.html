Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CarScene collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app.

Last updated: March 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use CarScene:

  • Email address, for account creation, sign-in, and account management
  • Username and profile-related information you choose to use in the app
  • App data such as cars seen, garages, collection progress, friends data, and saved preferences
  • Basic technical information required for authentication, cloud syncing, security, and app functionality

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Save your progress
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Provide app functionality, including account and social features
  • Let you connect with friends and view supported profile information
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the CarScene experience

3. Social Features

CarScene includes optional social features such as adding friends, viewing profiles, and comparing collections.

When you use these features, certain information may be visible to other users within the app, including:

  • Your username
  • Your collection value
  • Your most expensive cars seen
  • Your most recent cars seen
  • Your selected dream garages

This information is shown as part of the app’s social and profile features. By using these features, you understand that this information may be visible to other users you connect with in the app.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored using Google Firebase services.

CarScene does not store photos on its own servers. If photos are used in the app, they remain on your device unless a future feature clearly states otherwise.

5. Data Sharing

CarScene does not sell your personal information. We do not trade or rent your data to third parties.

Information may be processed by trusted service providers only as needed to operate the app, such as Firebase for authentication and cloud data storage.

In addition, certain profile and collection information may be visible to other users inside the app when you use social features.

6. Third-Party Services

CarScene uses third-party services that may process limited information in order to provide app functionality, including:

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Google Firebase Firestore or related Firebase cloud services

7. Your Choices

You may choose not to use social features, although some profile and friends functionality may then be unavailable.

You may also choose not to create an account, although some features such as cloud backup, syncing, friends, and profile functionality may then be unavailable.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data through the app or by contacting us.

8. Children’s Privacy

CarScene is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission where required by law.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised accordingly.
