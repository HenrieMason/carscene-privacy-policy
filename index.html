Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CarScene collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app.

Last updated: March 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Email address for account creation and authentication
  • Username and profile information
  • App data such as cars seen, garages, and collection progress
  • Friends and social interaction data
  • Basic technical data needed for app functionality and security

2. How We Use Information

  • Save your progress
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Enable social features and friend connections
  • Provide and improve the app experience

3. Social Features

CarScene includes optional social features such as searching for users, sending friend requests, and viewing profiles.

Your username may be searchable within the app so that other users can send you a friend request.

If you accept a friend request, the following information may be visible to that friend:

  • Your username
  • Your collection value
  • Your most expensive cars seen
  • Your most recent cars seen
  • Your selected dream garages

This information is only shared after you accept a friend request.

You can control whether information is shared by choosing whether to accept or decline friend requests.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored using Google Firebase services.

Photos remain on your device unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

5. Data Sharing

CarScene does not sell your personal information.

Data may be processed by trusted services such as Firebase for authentication and storage.

Profile and collection data may be visible to other users only through accepted friend connections.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Google Firebase Firestore

7. Your Choices

You may choose not to use social features.

You may request account and data deletion at any time.

8. Children’s Privacy

CarScene does not knowingly collect personal information from children without required permission.

9. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted here with a revised date.
